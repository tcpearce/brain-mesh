# Brain mesh demo

**Topic:** Temperature and solvent effects on analyte binding by imprinted polymer films (invented example data)

## Overview
This is a demonstration vault built from two invented papers and one invented own result. It shows the card formats, the links and the concept hubs, not real science.

What it says: binding capacity of the imprinted film falls about threefold on heating in water and recovers on cooling, but does not change in acetonitrile. Reversible swelling in water is the supported explanation. Whether the film swells at all in acetonitrile has never been measured.
