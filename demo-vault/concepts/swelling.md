# Swelling

**Definition.** Expansion of the polymer network as it takes up solvent.

## Relationships
- Proposed to distort the imprinted cavities and so lower **[[binding-capacity]]** ([[P001-007-swelling-distorts-cavities|claim, Smith & Jones 2021]]). Never tested directly.
