# Temperature

**Definition.** Temperature of the liquid in contact with the film during binding.

## What is known
- Both sources span 25 to 45 °C only; nothing is known outside that range.

## Relationships
- Raising it lowers **[[binding-capacity]]** in water ([[K001-capacity-falls-on-heating-in-water|finding K001]]) but not in acetonitrile ([[X001-does-heat-or-solvent-uptake-lower-capacity|tension X001]]).
- Raising it increases **[[film-thickness]]** in water ([[P001-005-film-thickens-18-percent|Smith & Jones 2021]]).
