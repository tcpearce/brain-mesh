# Is the loss of binding capacity on heating in water reversible on cooling?

**Unknown.** Whether [[binding-capacity|binding capacity]] recovers when a film heated to 45 °C in [[water]] is cooled back to 25 °C.

## Why it matters
Reversible loss points to [[swelling]]; permanent loss points to damage to the cavities. It decides whether heated sensors can be reused.

## What is known
The authors raise the question themselves ([[P001-011-reversibility-on-cooling-unknown|Smith & Jones 2021]]). No source in the brain reports a cooling step.

## Search log
- 2026-11-20 closed by our own experiment before any literature search: capacity recovers on cooling ([[P003-003-capacity-recovers-on-cooling|own result]]).

## What would close it
A heat-then-cool cycle on the same films with capacity read at each step.
