# Film thickness has never been measured in acetonitrile, so swelling is not ruled in or out there

**Unknown.** Whether the [[imprinted-polymer-film|film]] swells at all in [[acetonitrile]] between 25 and 45 °C.

## Why it matters
The swelling explanation ([[H001-loss-is-reversible-swelling|hypothesis H001]]) predicts no thickening in acetonitrile. Without the measurement the explanation rests on water alone.

## What is known
[[film-thickness|Thickness]] rose 18% in water ([[P001-005-film-thickens-18-percent|Smith & Jones 2021]]). The acetonitrile study reports none ([[P002-garcia-2023-binding-retained-acetonitrile|Garcia & Chen 2023]], fully extracted).

## Search log
- 2026-11-20 extraction check: both sources fully extracted; no thickness value in acetonitrile.
- 2026-11-20 literature: two databases, "imprinted film swelling acetonitrile temperature", 9 hits screened, none measured thickness against temperature.

## What would close it
Ellipsometry of the same films in both solvents at 25 and 45 °C.
