# Films were spin coated at 2000 rpm and cured for 12 h

The [[imprinted-polymer-film|imprinted polymer films]] were prepared by spin coating at 2000 rpm and then cured for 12 hours. No film thickness, monomer composition or cure temperature is given.

**Source:** Smith & Jones 2021, Methods, p. 1 · [[P001_marked.pdf#page=1&annotation=69R|open page]]
