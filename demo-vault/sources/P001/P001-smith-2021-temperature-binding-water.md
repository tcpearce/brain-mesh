# Smith & Jones (2021) Temperature dependence of analyte X binding by an imprinted polymer film

**Citation.** A. Smith, B. Jones. *Journal of Test Materials* 12, 100–108 (2021). doi:10.0000/jtm.2021.001 · [[P001.pdf|PDF]] · [[P001_text|anchored text]]

**What they did.** Measured the [[binding-capacity|binding capacity]] of an [[imprinted-polymer-film|imprinted polymer film]] for [[analyte-x|analyte X]] in [[water]] at 25 and 45 °C by batch rebinding, alongside film thickness.

**What they found.** Capacity fell from 4.2 to 1.3 on heating while the film thickened by 18%. They attribute the loss to swelling.

**Weaknesses.** Water only; three films per condition; swelling is inferred from thickness, not tested as a cause.

**Extraction.** Every section extracted in full. No figures, tables or supplementary information.
