# Binding capacity for analyte X is 3.7 at 45 °C in acetonitrile

At 45 °C in [[acetonitrile]], the [[binding-capacity|binding capacity]] of the [[imprinted-polymer-film|imprinted polymer film]] for [[analyte-x|analyte X]] was 3.7 ± 0.3 (four films).

**Basis:** [[P002-001-capacity-measured-by-qcm|quartz crystal microbalance]].
**Source:** Garcia & Chen 2023, Results, p. 1 · [[P002_marked.pdf#page=1&annotation=58R|open page]]
