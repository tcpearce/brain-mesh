# P002 claims: temperature alone does not degrade the imprinted cavities

**Main claim.** Heating alone does not degrade the imprinted cavities: in [[acetonitrile]] the [[binding-capacity|binding capacity]] is unchanged between 25 and 45 °C ([[P002-005-heat-alone-does-not-degrade-cavities|P002-005]], [[P002-004-no-change-on-heating-in-acetonitrile|P002-004]]).

**Sub-claims**
1. Binding capacity is 3.9 at 25 °C and 3.7 at 45 °C in acetonitrile, a difference within error ([[P002-002-capacity-3-9-at-25c-acetonitrile|P002-002]], [[P002-003-capacity-3-7-at-45c-acetonitrile|P002-003]]).
2. The loss reported in [[water]] comes from water uptake rather than heating ([[P002-006-water-uptake-causes-loss-in-water|P002-006]]).

**How far the evidence carries it.** The acetonitrile result is measured, but on one film thickness ([[P002-007-single-film-thickness-tested|P002-007]]) and with a different assay from the water study. Sub-claim 2 is offered as a suggestion; the paper measures nothing in water.
