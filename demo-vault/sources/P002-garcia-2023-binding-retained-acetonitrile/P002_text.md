Imprinted polymer films retain analyte X binding at elevated temperature in acetonitrile C. Garcia and D. Chen. Polymer Sensing Letters 4, 55-60 (2023). doi:10.0000/psl.2023.014 ^b0001

Abstract Using a quartz crystal microbalance we show that imprinted polymer films keep their binding capacity for analyte X when heated in acetonitrile. ^b0002

Results In acetonitrile the binding capacity was 3.9 ± 0.4 at 25 °C and 3.7 ± 0.3 at 45 °C (n = 4), with no significant change (p = 0.41). ^b0003

Discussion Temperature alone does not degrade the imprinted cavities. We suggest that the loss reported by Smith and Jones in water arises from water uptake by the film rather than from heating. Only one film thickness, 200 nm, was tested. Swelling of hydrogels with temperature is well documented (Lee 2015). ^b0004
