# Garcia & Chen (2023) Imprinted polymer films retain analyte X binding at elevated temperature in acetonitrile

**Citation.** C. Garcia, D. Chen. *Polymer Sensing Letters* 4, 55–60 (2023). doi:10.0000/psl.2023.014 · [[P002.pdf|PDF]] · [[P002_text|anchored text]]

**What they did.** Measured [[binding-capacity|binding capacity]] of [[imprinted-polymer-film|imprinted polymer films]] for [[analyte-x|analyte X]] in [[acetonitrile]] at 25 and 45 °C with a [[quartz-crystal-microbalance|quartz crystal microbalance]].

**What they found.** No significant change in capacity on heating. They argue that heat alone does not damage the cavities and that the loss seen in water comes from water uptake.

**Weaknesses.** One film thickness; a different measurement method from the water study, so solvent and method are confounded.

**Extraction.** Every section extracted in full. No figures, tables or supplementary information.
