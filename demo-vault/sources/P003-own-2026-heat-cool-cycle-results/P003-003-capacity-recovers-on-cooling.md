# Own films: binding capacity recovers to 3.8 after cooling back to 25 °C in water

After heating to 45 °C and cooling back to 25 °C in [[water]], the [[binding-capacity|binding capacity]] recovered to 3.8 ± 0.3, within error of its starting value of 4.0.
