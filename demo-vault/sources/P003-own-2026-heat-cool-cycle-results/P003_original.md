Own experiment E001, run 2026-11-18. Films from batch 7, batch rebinding assay with UV detection, n = 4 films per condition.

In water the binding capacity was 4.0 ± 0.3 at 25 °C and 1.4 ± 0.2 at 45 °C. After cooling back to 25 °C the capacity recovered to 3.8 ± 0.3.

In acetonitrile the binding capacity was 3.9 ± 0.3 at 25 °C and 3.8 ± 0.3 at 45 °C.
