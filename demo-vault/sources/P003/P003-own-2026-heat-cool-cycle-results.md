# Own result (2026) Heat-then-cool cycle in water and acetonitrile, experiment E001

**Citation.** Own experiment, run 2026-11-18, films from batch 7. Notes: [[P003_original|original record]] · [[P003_text|anchored text]]

**What was done.** The registered design of [[E001-heat-cool-cycle-two-solvents|experiment E001]]: batch rebinding, four films per condition, two solvents, 25 → 45 → 25 °C.

**What was found.** In water capacity fell from 4.0 to 1.4 and recovered to 3.8 on cooling. In acetonitrile it did not change.
