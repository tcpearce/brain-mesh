# Does heating itself lower binding capacity, or only heating in water?

**The disagreement.** Capacity falls on heating in [[water]] but not in [[acetonitrile]]. The two studies also differ in measurement method, so [[solvent]] and method are confounded.

## Sides
- Heating lowers capacity through swelling: [[P001-007-swelling-distorts-cavities|Smith & Jones 2021]].
- Heat alone does nothing; the solvent matters: [[P002-005-heat-alone-does-not-degrade-cavities|Garcia & Chen 2023]].

## Possible explanations
1. Water uptake rises with [[temperature]] and distorts the cavities; acetonitrile does not swell the film.
2. The two methods respond differently: batch rebinding may under-read if equilibrium is slower at 45 °C.

## What would settle it
Both solvents, both temperatures, one method, with [[film-thickness|thickness]] measured in each condition.
